# Phase 0 Research: Part B Presentation

## Decision 1: AI scope
**Decision**: Focus on Generative AI in enterprise decision-making.
**Rationale**: Narrow scope fits the 10-slide limit while enabling depth on ethics, security, and governance in a business context.
**Alternatives considered**: Broad AI scope; AI in healthcare; AI in cybersecurity.

## Decision 2: Governance frameworks
**Decision**: Use NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) and GDPR.
**Rationale**: AI RMF provides AI-specific risk governance; GDPR covers privacy and data protection obligations relevant to enterprise GenAI.
**Alternatives considered**: GDPR only; EU AI Act + GDPR; ISO/IEC 27001 + AI RMF.

## Decision 3: Case-study demonstration domain
**Decision**: Anchor the demonstration in AI for circular economy (cybersecurity + sustainability).
**Rationale**: The provided peer-reviewed article directly connects AI, cybersecurity risks, and governance frameworks, enabling an evidence-based walkthrough.
**Alternatives considered**: AI in finance (fraud); GenAI in HR; GenAI in customer support.

## Decision 4: Case study count
**Decision**: Include exactly two case studies.
**Rationale**: Meets requirement for real-world evidence while preserving slide budget and time limit.
**Alternatives considered**: One case study (too thin); three case studies (too dense for 10 slides).

## Decision 5: Source count
**Decision**: Use exactly eight peer-reviewed sources.
**Rationale**: Satisfies the minimum requirement without overloading slides and transcript.
**Alternatives considered**: More than eight sources (risk of overcrowding); fewer than eight (non-compliant).

## Decision 6: Demonstration format
**Decision**: Case-study walkthrough (step-by-step narrative).
**Rationale**: Matches the AI in circular economy evidence and supports a clear, concise demonstration segment.
**Alternatives considered**: Conceptual diagram-only demo; tool-based live demo.
