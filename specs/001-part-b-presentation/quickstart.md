# Quickstart: Part B Presentation

## Goal
Create a ≤10-slide, ≤15-minute presentation on Generative AI in enterprise decision-making, with a transcript and exactly eight peer-reviewed sources.

## Steps

1. Draft a slide outline (≤10 slides) covering: technical aspects, applications, ethical concerns, security risks, governance (NIST AI RMF + GDPR), and two case studies (including AI in circular economy).
2. Build the slide deck with concise bullets or visuals (≤40 words per slide) and in-text citations.
3. Write the transcript aligned to each slide, mirroring the slide citations.
4. Ensure the demonstration segment is the AI-in-circular-economy case study walkthrough.
5. Validate constraints: slide count, time limit, citation count, and case study count.

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] ≤10 slides
- [ ] ≤15 minutes
- [ ] ≤40 words per slide
- [ ] ≥1 visual per slide
- [ ] Font size ≥24pt and contrast ≥4.5:1
- [ ] Exactly 8 peer-reviewed sources
- [ ] Two case studies included (AI circular economy + one other)
- [ ] NIST AI RMF and GDPR covered
- [ ] Citations appear in slides and transcript
- [ ] Harvard (Cite Them Right) citation style used
- [ ] Slide-to-transcript overlap ≤20%
- [ ] Audio quality: ≤-20 dB noise floor, no clipping, MP3/WAV
