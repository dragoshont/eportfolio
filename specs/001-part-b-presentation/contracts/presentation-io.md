# Contract: Presentation Inputs/Outputs

## Inputs

- **Topic scope**: Generative AI in enterprise decision-making
- **Governance frameworks**: NIST AI RMF and GDPR
- **Case studies**: Exactly two (one is AI in circular economy)
- **Sources**: Exactly eight peer-reviewed sources
- **Constraints**: ≤10 slides, ≤15 minutes, ≤40 words per slide

## Outputs

### Slide Deck (≤10 slides)

Each slide must include:
- Slide number and title
- 2–4 concise bullets or visuals
- In-text citations (author, year)
- Word count ≤40

### Transcript (≤15 minutes)

For each slide:
- Slide number reference
- Narration aligned to slide bullets
- In-text citations matching slide citations

## Validation Checklist

- Slide count ≤10
- Duration ≤15 minutes
- Exactly 8 peer-reviewed sources cited on slides and in transcript
- Two case studies included
- Governance frameworks explicitly covered
- Demonstration segment is the AI-in-circular-economy case study walkthrough
